Worlds to Discover

Minecraft worlds contain different environments. Each one can offer its own scenery, resources and building opportunities.

Green landscapes

Forest and plains

These areas are useful places to begin exploring, gather basic materials and plan a starter base.

High ground

Mountains

Mountainous areas offer dramatic views and interesting locations for a lookout or hillside base.

Dry environment

Desert

Desert landscapes have a distinct appearance and can make an interesting setting for a themed build.

Water and shore

Oceans and beaches

Coastal areas are great inspiration for docks, boats, seaside homes and exploration routes.

Plan an exploration trip

  1. Choose an area you want to explore.
  2. Carry suitable supplies and leave room for collected items.
  3. Keep track of the route back to your base.
  4. Record interesting locations for future building projects.

The descriptions above are general gameplay ideas. Specific world generation and available features can vary by game edition and version.
